01 / INFORMATION

Business information

When to contact us

Before starting cooperation, granting a payment deferral, or when you want to monitor changes in an existing partner's position.

What we do

  • Credit reports
  • Company monitoring
  • Market research and press reviews

How we start

Tell us which company you are interested in, its country of registration and the question you need answered. We will then agree on the information required.

02 / DEBT MANAGEMENT

Debt prevention and recovery

When to contact us

When payments are overdue, you need to assess a debtor's solvency or agree on how obligations will be settled.

What we do

  • Debtor solvency assessment
  • Pre-trial debt recovery
  • Negotiations and payment schedules

Information in context

Assess a partner
from several angles.

A single figure is often not enough to make a decision. Our report combines information from different sources so that you can better understand a company's situation.

Enquire about a report ↗
The company and its connections

Registration details, activities, shareholders, management and related companies.

Financial position

Financial statements, ratios and credit rating, giving an overall picture of solvency.

Legal and reputational information

Court records, asset seizures, press coverage and information on international sanctions.

Change monitoring

Changes in registration details, debts and other relevant data of selected companies, tracked at an agreed frequency.

The scope of a report depends on the country, data availability and the agreed terms of the order.

About BVS Info

Trust begins
with responsible work.

UAB „BVS Info“ has been operating since 1996. We provide business information and debt recovery services and, since 2024, personal and property security services under a licence issued in Lithuania. The company's activities are covered by civil liability insurance.

We take a tailored approach to each client and work with partners in Lithuania and other countries. It is important to us to understand your situation and offer the right support.
